About Abyan

Founded in November 2015, Abyan Mgt. Services Corp. set out to bridge the relationship between labor and capital. We're a corporation duly registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission, and a legitimate service contractor under DOLE Department Order No. 174. As we grow, innovation in how we work will be continuous — but the dignity of work and the rights of our workers will always come first.
